## Team Context

The **Experience Design team** shapes the end-to-end customer experience across Zopa's products. Within it, the **Design Systems function** provides the foundations that allow product teams to move quickly while delivering consistent, high-quality experiences.

Zopa recently rebuilt their design system as part of a move to native — providing a strong foundation for the future. This role leads the **next phase**: owning the design system as a strategic platform to scale delivery, improve consistency, strengthen governance, and unlock AI-assisted design and development workflows.

The role leads a small team and works closely with Product, Engineering and Design leaders.

---

## Role: Design Systems Lead

### A Day in the Life

- Lead and develop a team consisting of a Design Systems Designer and Motion Designer
- Define and evolve the governance, processes and operating model for Zopa's design system
- Partner closely with Mobile Engineering to ensure design and code systems remain aligned
- Collaborate with Product, Design and Brand stakeholders on system adoption and evolution
- Review and prioritise requests for new patterns, components and system enhancements
- Define and track measures of system health, adoption, consistency and impact
- Help product teams move faster by creating scalable, reusable foundations
- Balance hands-on design systems work with strategic leadership and organisational influence
- Shape how AI-enabled workflows can leverage the design system to improve delivery speed and quality
- Drive consistency across customer experiences while making thoughtful trade-offs where needed
