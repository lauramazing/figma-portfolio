# Designer Advocate — Figma (London, United Kingdom)

**Location:** London, England  
**Type:** Full-time, hybrid (London hub)

---

Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma's platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us!

Our Advocacy team drives this mission — building impactful content, high-value interactions, and a strong user voice. We're looking for a Designer Advocate to partner closely with our Marketing, Sales, and Product teams. You'll bring deep design expertise, strong product intuition, and a genuine love for the design community into our go-to-market strategies, while championing our users' voices internally to help shape our product evolution.

---

## What you'll do at Figma

**Empower users at scale**  
Collaborate closely with Sales and Customer Experience teams to help customers unlock the full potential of Figma across real product workflows.

**Create compelling content**  
Develop written, visual, and video resources that reflect how modern teams actually design and build, from early exploration to production-ready systems.

**Inspire community connections**  
Foster meaningful relationships within the Figma community, identify collaboration opportunities, and nurture user-driven initiatives.

**Speak and engage at events**  
Regularly present at industry meetups, conferences, and community events, sharing practical insights from real-world design work.

**Champion user voices**  
Stay attuned to community feedback, sentiment, and emerging design trends — advocating internally to inform product decisions and improvements.

---

## We'd love to hear from you if you have

- Deep, hands-on Figma expertise and an entrepreneurial drive, with experience integrating Figma within real product teams and a track record of taking ideas from early exploration to impactful, polished outcomes
- A thorough understanding of modern product design workflows and development environments, with experience building, managing, and using design systems, tokens, and AI-assisted design or prototyping, while collaborating closely with engineers and other cross-functional partners
- Clear and compelling communication skills, especially when explaining complex workflows or systems, with confidence speaking publicly and engaging large audiences
- Strong technical design skills and an understanding of how design decisions translate into development
- A passion for the design community, eagerness to engage and contribute, and comfort travelling up to 25% of the time to connect with that community in person

---

## Nice to have

- Experience working with developer handoff tools or code-connected design workflows
