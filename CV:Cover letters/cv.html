Career highlights include

  • Researching design-to-development collaboration across 100+ developers in 12 countries — identifying the cultural root cause, publishing guidance, and establishing practices that brought design and engineering into shared ownership earlier.
  • Leading a team of designers to migrate 8 newly acquired products onto a shared design system in 9 months — defining the governance model, influencing teams who hadn't chosen the system, and building the compliance framework used to report progress to senior leadership.
  • Contributing many components and patterns to Carbon alongside my day job in product — including button guidance, full page errors (identified through an accessibility contrast gap in dark mode), tag overflow and grid influencers — identifying gaps through product work and driving them through the formal contribution process into system-level standards used across IBM.

  • Designing an AI-driven radiology triage system — filed as a US patent in 2022 — applying machine learning to surface the most critical cases first, before AI tooling was a mainstream conversation in product.
  • Building IBM's Product Gallery — making good design visible and reusable across IBM's global design community.
  • Mentoring 10+ designers across my career — on design systems, contribution processes, and visual design craft.
