The story

Making good design visible

IBM Software spans thousands of designers across hundreds of products. Good design was happening everywhere — but teams had no way to see it. Design systems gave teams the rules; no one could see how others had applied them.

I interviewed 19 designers to validate the idea — 11 out of 19 gave it 5/5 for value. Teams wanted hi-fi screens and end-to-end flows for inspiration and reuse. The gallery had been wanted for years but never launched. I led the initiative alongside a colleague to finally ship it.

The solution was Airtable — self-service submission, no engineering dependency, iframed directly onto the design system site. It scaled to 56 product submissions and 144 live screens without requiring ongoing maintenance from our volunteer team.

Filling gaps in the design system

Carbon is IBM's open-source design system — used across hundreds of IBM products and by teams across the industry. All of my contributions happened outside my day job.

I partnered with Carbon's core systems team to author guidelines, build Figma library components, and establish standards adopted upstream into the system. Over four years I contributed grid components, menu and combo buttons, accessible multi-theme error views, and tag overflow behaviours — translating community feedback directly into scalable system standards.

Grid influencers: Standardizing fixed layouts

Grid components for Carbon's navigation and persistent rails — adopted into Carbon's core 2x Grid guidelines ↗.

Button guidance & Figma kit additions

Authored placement and hierarchy specs, and built menu and combo buttons directly into the Figma kit to give designers clear action patterns across SaaS products.

Tag overflow & content truncation

Designed uniform tag overflow behavior to govern multi-attribute profiles, dense data grids, and localized truncations, eliminating custom hacks across hundreds of production screens.

Full page errors: Light & dark mode compliance

Led a design-to-code sprint building flexible, high-contrast, multi-theme error illustrations that resolved critical WCAG contrast failures across Security products.
